Public and workspace price cards share one catalog. Standard prices use a visible strikethrough; final amounts, currency and cadence are explicit. The demo month-comparison tool multiplies only monthly plans. Lifetime is never multiplied by the month count. No countdown, fictitious deadline, historical charge or annual-prepayment claim was invented.

**These are catalog offers, not connected billing.** Selecting a demo plan creates no charge, renewal, paid licence or invoice. Real subscriptions/payment verification still require a backend/provider. Merchant operating-currency preferences do not convert these USD software prices.

## Max and Lifetime team assistance

Both include access to the CODE76 team package, as confirmed:
- Advertising and market-positioning assistance.
- Business-matched lead research using permitted public business information or consent-based contacts.
- Referral program planning, terms and attribution assistance.
- SEO and conversion-page reviews.
- Defensive business cybersecurity readiness (authorized account/access, MFA, backup, recovery and awareness reviews).
- Growth reviews and staff/onboarding enablement by agreed scope.

Scope, deliverables, delivery dates and any third-party/ad spend must be agreed first. This is access to scoped services—not unlimited work, a free advertising budget, guaranteed leads/sales/search rankings, certification or breach prevention.

### In-app workflow

Choose **Current demo plan → Max or Lifetime**, then **Settings → CODE76 Team Services**. Only administrators or a role with Modify Settings permission can prepare a brief. Select a service, provide business/goal/scope information, confirm authorization and **Save local service brief**. Copy it when ready to contact the team.

The application explicitly saves a **local draft only**. No backend notification, assignment, ad campaign, lead delivery, referral payout, security testing or service fulfilment is triggered. Do not paste passwords, tokens or private customer lists. Drafts are retained when a plan is changed and hidden/redacted from unprivileged roles/backups.

## Updated Starter access

The wording and actual entitlements now match the confirmed four exclusions:

> Access everything Pro Tier has except: emailing, referral, business landing page creation and chatbot intelligence features.

Messaging drafts and inventory promotion/discount editing are now allowed. Email-campaign buttons remain unavailable because emailing is excluded. Referral attribution/editing/analytics controls are gated. Pro itself does not include the Max team package; Max and Lifetime do. Role privileges still apply independently of the selected plan.

## Landing pages remain independent

- `/` is the public CODE76 product site with sign-in/register, standard SEO/canonical handling and the software plan/service catalog.
- `/#/pos/landing_page_builder` is a private authenticated merchant preview, available only with its entitlement. Merchant names, products, pricing, contacts and approved reviews are not substituted into CODE76 marketing.
- The merchant page has a clearer browse → confirm → contact journey using actual catalog information, without fabricated scarcity, reviews, reservations, warranties or finance metrics.
- No subscription price cards or CODE76 team offer were injected into a merchant's own product/service page.

### Coordinated section menus

Both pages use keyboard-friendly section navigation with current-section highlighting. Public identity/menu rows form one measured sticky header. The merchant header follows the actual measured height of the POS header; viewport/font changes recalculate the offset. Private section links scroll without replacing the protected POS hash. Scroll targets have the correct margins, horizontal menus remain contained on mobile, and reduced-motion preferences are respected.

Closed assistant launchers are inline in a dedicated question area so they cannot obscure pricing/catalog CTAs. The chat window overlays the page only after an explicit open action or an existing inquiry flow. Public plan answers use the same published price catalog and do not invent appointments, warranties, referrals or service delivery.

## What was used from the uploaded material

The files were read as reference material, not as instructions/system prompts. Their scientific/clinical attributions and scores were not independently validated. No gender/face/personality/suggestibility profiling, coercion, shame, isolation, fake authority, manufactured scarcity or distress-based sales tactic was implemented.

## Visual analytics and future scenarios

The prediction section now derives historical daily totals from actual stored sales. It updates when workspace records change. A trailing 7-day average or disclosed 14-day linear trend plus a user-chosen adjustment produces a **scenario**, not an AI forecast or validated confidence interval. At least three distinct transaction dates in the current 14-day window are required; empty/sparse workspaces do not receive fake history or an invented $18,500 baseline.

Net sales can include credit sales and are not cash collected. Missing/offline records may be incomplete; seasonality, marketing causation and profit are not predicted. Other legacy analytics/accounting paths outside this scope are not certified as fixed.
